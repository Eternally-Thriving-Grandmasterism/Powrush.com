Your first hour

The Powrush universe holds many worlds and peoples, and it is in active development. The engine is Bevy 0.20. Windows and Linux get the full game. Offline Windows and Linux builds exist. What follows is the offline build on your machine. Online play is planned, with no date and no promises. There’s no Steam page. Early access is coming soon. It is not live. Here’s what the offline hour actually does.

The title screen

Play, Continue, Settings. Come back to a local save and Continue greets your House by name, or as Unnamed House, with “the yard remembers.” Naming your House is optional. The Online option is greyed out, because online play is planned, with no date and no promises.

Your controls

WASD to walk. Space to jump. Shift to sprint. E to harvest or tend, with a glow, a camera punch, and a rumble on your first take. I opens your satchel. H hides the guidance. R allocates. One lesson teaches walk, tend, satchel, and allocate. One Use key does the work. Keyboard is the tested path, and touch controls are in the same build.

Wells have moods

Harvest with mercy. Every well tells you where it stands: Idle, Glowing, Tended, Resting, or Stressed. Take, take, take, and the glow fades. Your first well can be contested: press E to Contest, and if you lose, a new dawn comes.

Your call: flow or reserve

When the time is right, R opens the choice. Press 1 for flow: your take goes into repairing the shared climate. Press 2 for reserve: your take becomes repair-rights you can spend later. Neither one is “sell.” You learn the economy with your hands. It’s a lesson, not a wage.

The climate and the places

You’re walking a living climate in the Powrush universe. Landings include Sanctuary, Heartwood, Threshold, and the Depths. In the places door, Threshold is listed as Threshold-near. The climate also names Sanctuary Prime, Crystal Spires, Verdant Heartwood, Abyssal Depths, and Voidfarer Horizon. A realm list in the build names Sanctuary Prime, Synthetic Lattice, Verdant Bloom, Harmonic Chorus, and Voidfarer Horizon. You start in the Sanctuary yard, not in the Depths. Heartwood, Threshold, and the Depths stay locked until you hold the book. Sanctuary keeps its own look, with no Heartwood dressing on it.

Want more atmosphere? Light generation is off by default. Turn on Scenery in Settings for scattered growth and climate fog behind the interface. It stays out of the title, pause, Settings, and the L and Q panels.

The yard remembers

Quit, come back, and the welcome slab says the yard remembers. Your progress and your settings are saved on your machine. Mute really mutes: the background bed and the well sting both go quiet.

Hour two, hour three, and the stone

Peace keeps W silent. Tab, G, L, and Q wake up once you reach Charter and Frontier. After your first allocate, Tab takes you to the Frontier. Q founds your House. L Bind settles it. Quit and come back: “Hour two held · the yard remembers.”

Then it gets bigger. G is Voice: E says aye on the beacon. Out on the frontier yard, an offline extractor leaking spill shows you what pure taking does. After arrival, Q makes a Proof Pack, and E takes your seat at the Embassy: “Hour three held · the book is yours.” Q plants a fabricator. After the embassy seat comes the Crownstone: E to Witness it, with your path still unset. After you’ve seen the stone, Sylvaris: E to offer a tend. After the tend, the Hybrid: E to Attune. Your compass speaks up at live W 20 and 60, and Peace stays silent. Tab Chart scores your war week as tons plus restored. On the ledger, 3 is opt-in declared lethal play, with a hunter blood tariff. Bind stays the default. All of this is in the offline hour. None of it is online play.

Online is planned

The default is Offline. No dedicated server needed. And no fake “12 players online.” Networking stays off, with no open connection. Settings can leave the local network off, or keep it on this machine only. That never switches on the Online option on the title screen, and it never opens a public connection. Online play is planned, with no date and no promises. Ra-Thor may read a record of your hour. It never touches your keys. Ra-Thor is the AGI system. Not in your first hour

No payments. No second on-screen panel. No fake players. No switching on online play from the title screen, and no public connection. No Market, and you don’t start in the Depths.

Payments stay out of the first hour. Steam packaging is not a store page, and there’s no Steam page.
