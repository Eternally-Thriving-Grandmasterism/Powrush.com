Your first hour

Powrush-MMO is in active development. The current build is offline and single-player. Online play isn’t available yet. There’s no Steam page and no release date, and it isn’t a public launch candidate. Here’s what’s actually in it.

The default game in the Powrush-MMO (opens in a new tab) repository is the first hour. Hours one and two are code-complete, and hours 1–3 are on main. Everything below comes from the README and the docs, and every card links its source file on main, so you can check us. The full plugin list is client/src/lib.rs (opens in a new tab).

The title screen

Play, Continue, Settings. Come back to a local save and Continue greets your House by name, or as Unnamed House, with “the yard remembers.” Naming your House is optional. The Online option is greyed out, because online play isn’t available yet.

  • client/src/title_screen.rs (opens in a new tab)

Your controls

WASD to walk. Space to jump. Shift to sprint. E to harvest or tend, with a glow, a camera punch, and a rumble on your first take. I opens your satchel. H hides the guidance. R allocates. One card teaches walk, tend, satchel, and allocate. One Use key does the work. Keyboard is the tested path, and touch controls are in the same client.

Wells have moods

Harvest with mercy. Every well tells you where it stands: Idle, Glowing, Tended, Resting, or Stressed. Take, take, take, and the glow fades. Your first well can be contested: press E to Contest, and if you lose, a new dawn comes.

Your call: flow or reserve

When the time is right, R opens the choice. Press 1 for flow: your take goes into repairing the shared climate. Press 2 for reserve: your take becomes repair-rights you can spend later. Neither one is “sell.” You learn the economy with your hands. It’s a lesson, not a wage.

  • client/src/rbe_allocate_choice.rs (opens in a new tab)

The climate and the places

You’re walking a living climate. Four places: Sanctuary, Heartwood, Threshold, and Depths. You start in the Sanctuary yard, not in Depths, and the other places stay locked until you hold the book. Sanctuary keeps its own look, with no Heartwood dressing on it.

Want more atmosphere? Light generation is off by default. Turn on Settings Grove · light, or set POWRUSH_GEN=light, for hex-seeded scatter and climate fog behind the UI. Same result either way. It stays out of the title, pause, Settings, and the L and Q panels.

The yard remembers

Quit, come back, and the welcome slab says the yard remembers. Your progress lives in data/powrush_hour_two.json, and your settings save right next to it. Mute really mutes: the background bed and the well sting both go quiet.

Hour two, hour three, and the stone

Peace keeps W silent. Tab, G, L, and Q wake up once you reach Charter and Frontier. After your first allocate, Tab takes you to the Frontier. Q founds your House. L Bind settles it. Quit and come back: “Hour two held · the yard remembers.”

Then it gets bigger. G is Voice: E says aye on the beacon card. Out on the frontier yard, an offline extractor leaking spill shows you what pure taking does. After arrival, Q makes a Proof Pack, and E takes your seat at the Embassy: “Hour three held · the book is yours.” Q plants a fabricator. After the embassy seat comes the Crownstone: E to Witness it, with your path still unset. After you’ve seen the stone, Sylvaris: E to offer a tend. After the tend, the Hybrid: E to Attune. Your compass speaks up at live W 20 and 60, and Peace stays silent. Tab Chart scores your war week as tons plus restored. Ledger 3 is opt-in: DeclaredLethal, with a hunter blood tariff. Bind stays the default. All of this is in the offline hour. None of it is online play.

Online? Not yet.

The default is Offline. No dedicated server needed. And no fake “12 players online.” POWRUSH_NET is off by default, with zero sockets. Settings LAN is off, or loopback, and loopback means 127.0.0.1 only. It never switches on the Online option on the title screen, and it never opens a public connection. Ra-Thor may read a record of your hour. It never touches your keys.

  • client/src/net_mode.rs (opens in a new tab)

Not in your first hour

No NFTs or chain mints. No payments. No second HUD. No fake players. No switching on online play from the title screen, and no public connection. No Market, and you don’t start in Depths.

Some code is parked on disk and isn’t part of the default game: server/, simulation/, host/, game/, powrush-divine-module/, and powrush-shard/. The shard listener is a local dev recipe only. payments/ stays out of the first hour. Steam packaging is parked, and there’s no Steam page.

Want the lore of the five peoples and four places? That’s on World. Play on this site is coming later.
