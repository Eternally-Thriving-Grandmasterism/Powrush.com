What you walk into

Powrush is a human game from Autonomicity Games Inc. (opens in a new tab). You walk a climate, harvest with mercy, and learn a resource-based economy by playing. The lesson is in the hands. The later hope is shared abundance, not extraction. It is a teaching, not a wage.

The yard is the universe at the scale of a well. One human, one machine. Offline first. Title Online stays grey. The current build is offline and single-player, and it is in active development. Online play is not live. There is no Steam page, and there is no release date. It is not a public launch candidate.

Powrush has been under development since 2016 by Autonomicity Games Inc. The public repository is Powrush-MMO (opens in a new tab).

The initials AGI are a deliberate play on artificial general intelligence, chosen when the company was founded in 2016.
