Here’s the deal

Step into the yard

The Powrush universe is from Autonomicity Games Inc. (Company website (opens in a new tab)). You walk a living climate. You harvest with mercy. You learn how a resource-based economy works by playing it, not by reading about it. Take too much and the place shows it. Share well and it heals. This isn’t a grind for a paycheck: the reward is a place that comes back to life.

The Powrush universe holds many worlds and peoples. This site, powrush.com, is the game site. acitygames.com is the company site, and rathor.ai is the AGI system site. Windows and Linux get the full game. Offline Windows and Linux builds exist. Early access is coming soon. It is not live. Online play is planned, with no date and no promises. There’s no Steam page. The engine is Bevy 0.20.

Powrush has been in the works since 2016 at Autonomicity Games Inc., according to the studio’s Powrush page (opens in a new tab). The licence carries a copyright of 2016–2026. Source on GitHub (opens in a new tab).

The universe, painted

These paintings show the Powrush universe as it’s imagined. They aren’t gameplay, and they aren’t screenshots of the current offline build. In the build itself, the look is gritty low-poly shapes and rich, detailed light: muted earth, plus one bold accent colour for each place.
