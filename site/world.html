After the end, a turn

The world looks a lot like Earth, after scarcity, fear, and separation nearly wrecked the web of life. Then came the Great Shift: a turn toward mercy, and toward a resource-based economy. That’s where you come in. The look on the surface is post-apocalyptic, and the design puts diplomacy first. Work together or compete. Keep the peace or start a war. The stakes go as high as nuclear war, so you can face that extreme safely inside a game.

It’s designed as an open world, where your story and your quests grow out of what you want. Powrush has been in the works since 2016 at Autonomicity Games Inc., as the studio’s Powrush page (opens in a new tab) says.

Five peoples

Pick your people. Under all five runs the same tug-of-war: harmony against consumption.

  • Human Survivors caught between two cosmic powers. “We endure. We choose. We become.”
  • Quellorian Masters of light, resonance, and unity. “Through harmony, we transcend.”
  • Draek A hivemind empire that consumes and dominates. “All will be one in the Brood.”
  • Cydruid A human in a cyborg frame. The frame is worn. The person remains.
  • Ambrosian Living crystal, tuned to harmony. “We are the note, the chord, the song.”

In the design, the faction system lets you be as diplomatic as you like. Two powers pull at you: a coalition of light, and an empire of consumption.

Light vs. the Brood

Quellorian / Aetherion Luminari Alliance

A coalition of advanced species built on light, resonance technology, unity, and always evolving forward. Their aim is harmony. Their mothership is the Auroral Unification Nexus (TAUN): a huge radial ship glowing with blue-white aurora, a symbol of hope and of power kept in order.

Draek Dominion

A predatory hivemind empire that rules through mind control, consumption, and overwhelming force. Their aim is the Brood. Their hub is the Brood Spire, a tall, dark megastructure. And set in the center of the Hivelord’s helmet is the Crownstone, a large purple crystal that glows. Remember that stone.

Ambrosian

Crystalline-organic beings who live harmony, literally. Their creed: Attunement over Domination. Tall, elongated and elegant, with crystal structures glowing inside their bodies. In the build, the Ambrosian accent is “prism cool.”

Cydruid

A Cydruid is a human in a cyborg frame. The frame is worn. The grove is practiced. The person remains. Cydruids mend and they offer, weaving living networks that heal, protect, and restore. “We do not conquer the wild. We become its most faithful expression.” Limbs first, then torso: part by part, any human can go all the way to fully cyborg. Machine parts mean fewer infections, and bionic powers. And no, they aren’t tree people. The person is not a tree. Nature is a practice, not a species. On the frame: teal and leaf-metal trim, metal accents on a human.

Where you land

Five peoples, four places. Humans and Ambrosians land in Sanctuary. Cydruids land in Heartwood. Quellorians land in Threshold. Draek land in Depths. Out in the offline hour, every well tells you how it’s doing: idle, glowing, tended, resting, or stressed.

These paintings show the world as it’s imagined. They aren’t gameplay, and they aren’t screenshots of the current offline build. In the build itself, the look is gritty low-poly shapes and rich, detailed light: muted earth, plus one bold accent colour for each place.

What’s in the build today

You start in the Sanctuary yard, not in Depths. The other places stay locked until you hold the book. Sanctuary stays its own place, with no Heartwood dressing on it. No Market.
