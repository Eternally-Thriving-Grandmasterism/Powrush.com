After the end, a turn

The Powrush universe holds many worlds and peoples. One of those worlds is a fractured Earth: magma islands under a scarred moon, after scarcity, fear, and separation nearly wrecked the web of life. Then came the Great Shift: a turn toward mercy, and toward a resource-based economy. That’s where you come in. The look on the surface is post-apocalyptic, and the design puts diplomacy first. Work together or compete. Keep the peace or start a war. The stakes go as high as nuclear war, so you can face that extreme safely inside a game.

It’s designed as an open world, where your story and your quests grow out of what you want. Powrush has been in the works since 2016 at Autonomicity Games Inc., as the studio’s Powrush page (opens in a new tab) says.

Five peoples

Five peoples are written here. Under each one runs the same tug-of-war: harmony against consumption.

  • Human Survivors caught between two cosmic powers, led by Warden Ronan Ashcroft. “We endure. We choose. We become.”
  • Quellorian Luminous ancient aliens, masters of light, resonance, and unity, whose lost homeworld had two suns. Led by High Luminar Solmaris Orynthe (sol-MAR-is). “Through harmony, we transcend.”
  • Draek Hard-plated chitin humanoids. The Draek Dominion, ruled by the Hivelord, consumes and dominates. “All will be one in the Brood.” Freed Draeks follow Vrendak the Unbound (VREN-dak).
  • Cydruid Druids who chose cyborg frames, led by Archdruid Orin Hollowell from a stalactite city in Earth’s hollows. They harvest with care. The frame is worn. The person remains.
  • Ambrosian Living crystal, tuned to harmony, led by Lyrisse, Voice of the Prism Choir. “We are the note, the chord, the song.”

In the design, the faction system lets you be as diplomatic as you like. Two powers pull at you: a coalition of light, and an empire of consumption.

Light vs. the Brood

Aetherion Luminari Alliance

An alliance open to everyone who is peaceful and willing to promote TOLC (True Original Lord Creator), whatever their people, rebel Draeks included. It is built on light, resonance technology, and unity, always evolving forward. At its heart are the Quellorians, one people among many, led by High Luminar Solmaris Orynthe, with Lyrisse, Voice of the Prism Choir, as co-voice. Their aim is harmony. Their mothership is the Auroral Unification Nexus (TAUN): a huge radial ship glowing with blue-white aurora, a symbol of hope and of power kept in order.

Draek Dominion

An empire of hard-plated chitin humanoids, bound as a hivemind, that rules through mind control, consumption, and overwhelming force. It answers to the Hivelord, whose true name is unknown. Their aim is the Brood. Their hub is the Brood Spire, a tall, dark megastructure. Set in the centre of the Hivelord’s helmet is the Crownstone, a large purple crystal that glows. Remember that stone. Not every Draek kneels. Vrendak the Unbound was once the Drainer, and he broke free. Freed Draeks follow him toward redemption.

Ambrosian

Crystalline-organic beings who live as harmony. Their creed is Attunement over Domination. They follow Lyrisse, Voice of the Prism Choir. They are tall, elongated, and elegant, with crystal structures glowing inside their bodies. In the build, the Ambrosian accent is “prism cool.”

Cydruid

Cydruids are druids who chose to become cyborgs: humans and machines, merged as one. The frame is worn. The grove is practiced. The person remains. Led by Archdruid Orin Hollowell, they live in an upside-down stalactite city deep in Earth’s hollows, always burrowing. They harvest thoughtfully and carefully, and that care has quietly put them ahead of the humans on the surface. Cydruids mend and they offer, weaving living networks that heal, protect, and restore. “We do not conquer the wild. We become its most faithful expression.” Limbs first, then torso: part by part, any human can go all the way to fully cyborg. Machine parts mean fewer infections, and they bring bionic powers. And no, they aren’t tree people. The person is not a tree. Nature is a practice, not a species. On the frame: teal and leaf-metal trim, metal accents on a human.

Where you land

The Powrush universe holds many worlds and peoples. Five peoples are written here. Humans and Ambrosians land in Sanctuary. Cydruids land in Heartwood. Quellorians land in Threshold. The places door calls that shelf Threshold-near. Draeks land in the Depths. Those landings are not a closed list. The climate names Sanctuary Prime, Crystal Spires, Verdant Heartwood, Abyssal Depths, and Voidfarer Horizon. A realm list in the build names Sanctuary Prime, Synthetic Lattice, Verdant Bloom, Harmonic Chorus, and Voidfarer Horizon. Resource biomes named in the build include Crystal Fields, Deep Caves, Ancient Forest, Sunken Springs, Knowledge Archives, and Rare Mineral Veins. The Cydruid home is a stalactite city in Earth’s hollows. It may become a place later. It is not a landing yet. There is no Market. Out in the offline hour, every well tells you how it’s doing: idle, glowing, tended, resting, or stressed.

These paintings show the Powrush universe as it’s imagined. They aren’t gameplay, and they aren’t screenshots of the current offline build. In the build itself, the look is gritty low-poly shapes and rich, detailed light: muted earth, plus one bold accent colour for each place.

What’s in the build today

You start in the Sanctuary yard, not in the Depths. Heartwood, Threshold, and the Depths stay locked until you hold the book. Sanctuary stays its own place, with no Heartwood dressing on it. No Market.
